# Visual lesson quality standard

Each released Reel gets **one individual learning guide**, matched to the lesson ID and video title. A guide must stand alone for someone who arrives from Instagram, while fitting the ordered 90-lesson curriculum.

## Required anatomy

1. **Title, ID, level, outcomes** — what the learner will actually understand or accomplish.
2. **Previously learned** — accurately summarize the immediately preceding lesson in one or two sentences, with a working link if public.
3. **Prerequisites** — brief definitions or specific live guides needed; avoid broken future links.
4. **Intuition** — beginner-friendly mental model, realistic analogy, and its limitations.
5. **Detailed explanation** — mechanisms, architecture and relevant mathematics, not a glossary-only summary.
6. **Visual walkthrough** — original diagrams with labeled flows, descriptive alt text and accompanying prose that works without the image.
7. **Practice** — runnable code with exact versions, commands and observed results, or a justified worked non-code exercise.
8. **Failure modes and trade-offs** — where the concept breaks, security/privacy caveats, alternatives and misconceptions.
9. **Exercises with answers** — at least one comprehension question and one application question.
10. **Primary sources and change notes** — official documentation/research, versions and access/review dates when important.
11. **Continue learning** — next lesson link *only after it is published*; otherwise link to `ROADMAP.md`.

There is no fixed word quota. Complex subjects may need several diagrams and a long guide. Quality is measured by the accuracy and completeness of the **scoped lesson objective**, not by superficial length. A single guide is not expected to exhaust an entire field.

## Publication verification

- [ ] Topic and lesson ID match the public catalog and corresponding Reel.
- [ ] Claims are independently checked against current authoritative documentation.
- [ ] Code/commands have real recorded checks; no fake output.
- [ ] Diagrams and explanations match one another and are screen-reader accessible.
- [ ] No third-party copyrighted imagery, logos or text is used without appropriate permission.
- [ ] Examples do not leak secrets or recommend unsafe defaults.
- [ ] All external and relative links work; all published-navigation links resolve.
- [ ] Human editorial approval is recorded in the private production tracker.
